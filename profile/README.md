# TechFanar (تيك فنار)

**Software & AI Engineering in Dubai | Makers of [Orivenn HR](https://orivenn.com)**

TechFanar is a Dubai software and AI engineering company building production business software, private AI systems, and bilingual English and Arabic platforms for companies across the Middle East.

## Products & Platforms
- **[Orivenn HR](https://orivenn.com):** All-in-one bilingual English and Arabic HR and payroll platform for multi-entity companies, featuring maker-checker approvals, structured employee records, documents, and effective-dated audit history.
- **[GateReady](https://gateready.techfanar.com):** Move permits and dispatch operations software for Dubai teams.

## Engineering Focus
- **Custom Business Software:** Internal tools, commercial ERPs, field execution apps, and bilingual customer-facing web platforms.
- **Private Company AI:** Citation-grounded retrieval and assistants running on client cloud, on-premises, or hosted by TechFanar with strict role boundaries.
- **Governed AI Agent Workflows:** Multi-step operational automation with audit logs and human sign-off.
- **Enterprise Integrations & SLMs:** Connecting ERP, HR, and finance systems and tuning small language models for focused company tasks.
